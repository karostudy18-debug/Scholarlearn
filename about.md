# About Scholar Learn

**Scholar Learn** is an independent blog about **abacus**, **mental math**, and **online learning**. Our mission is simple: make abacus education accessible to every student, parent, and teacher — no matter where they live or what budget they have.

## Why We Started This Blog

Abacus training is one of the most effective ways to build mathematical ability in children. Research shows it improves number sense, concentration, working memory, and mental arithmetic speed. But high-quality abacus education is often locked behind expensive franchise programs and proprietary curricula.

We believe this knowledge should be free. Every guide on Scholar Learn is published openly, with no paywall, no subscription, and no upsell.

## What We Cover

- **Beginner guides** — what an abacus is, how to read beads, how to start practicing
- **Learning plans** — structured daily routines from 10 minutes to competition level
- **History and science** — how the abacus evolved over 5,000 years and why it still works
- **Tool reviews** — honest, independent reviews of abacus tools, apps, and kits
- **Hindi content** — full tutorials in Hindi for the Indian abacus community

## Why Abacus Training Works

The abacus is not just a calculator. It is a **brain training tool** that develops skills no other method can match:

- **Number sense** — understanding how numbers relate to each other
- **Concentration** — the ability to focus on a task for extended periods
- **Working memory** — holding and manipulating information mentally
- **Mental visualization** — seeing numbers and operations as images in your mind
- **Processing speed** — performing calculations faster than any calculator

Studies from Japan, China, India, and the United States consistently show that children who practice abacus outperform their peers in mathematics, science, and standardized tests.
