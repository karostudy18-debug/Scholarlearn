# Master Abacus, Master Math.

**Scholar Learn** is a free resource hub dedicated to **abacus training**, **mental math**, and **online learning tools** for students, parents, and educators worldwide.

We publish in-depth guides, historical deep-dives, and practical tutorials — from what an abacus is and who invented it, to how modern abacus apps and online tools make practice easier than ever. Our guides reference free tools like [AbacusTool](https://abacustool.xyz/) for hands-on practice.

## What You Will Find Here

- **Beginner guides** — step-by-step tutorials on learning the abacus from scratch
- **History** — how the abacus shaped mathematics and modern computers over 5,000 years
- **Tool reviews** — honest looks at online abacus tools, apps, and physical kits
- **Practice routines** — daily training plans that produce real results
- **Hindi guides** — full abacus tutorials in Hindi for Indian students and parents
